# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a place where people can ask questions, report bugs and send patches without friction.

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Keep discussion on topic and give feedback that is specific and actionable.
- Don't publish other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may ban people who
repeatedly do. To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on the Security tab and mark it as a conduct report,
or contact a maintainer directly.

This text is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
